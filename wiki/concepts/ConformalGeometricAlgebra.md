# Conformal Geometric Algebra

The generalization beyond [ProjectiveGeometricAlgebra](ProjectiveGeometricAlgebra.md). CGA can be seen as an **extension of PGA that adds circles and spheres** as fundamental objects, alongside lines and planes.

- Reflecting across circles and spheres gives **circle inversions**.
- Any **conformal transformation** can be represented with rotors — a strictly larger class than the rigid transformations PGA handles. See [Rotor](Rotor.md), [RigidTransformation](RigidTransformation.md).

## Historical note

CGA was discovered **before** PGA, and when PGA first appeared it was regarded as a *subset* of CGA — the reverse of the pedagogical order used here, where PGA comes first and CGA is the extension.
