# Join

The smallest object containing the inputs — the line through two points, the plane through a point and a line, the plane through three points. In [ProjectiveGeometricAlgebra](ProjectiveGeometricAlgebra.md) the join **is the [RegressiveProduct](RegressiveProduct.md)**, in every case and every dimension.

It is the exact dual of the [Meet](Meet.md): meet is the outer product, join is the regressive product, and neither changes shape when you add a dimension.
