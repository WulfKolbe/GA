# Keenan Crane

Author of [conformal-geometry-of-simplicial-surfaces](../sources/conformal-geometry-of-simplicial-surfaces.md), the survey of discrete conformal geometry ingested into this wiki. Affiliated with **Carnegie Mellon University** (5000 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh). The work was supported in part by NSF Award #1717268 and a Packard Fellowship.

## What the source shows of his own work

- **Digital geometry processing with discrete exterior calculus** (Crane, de Goes, Desbrun, Schröder) — the course notes he cites for derivations of the conformal energy.
- **A glimpse into discrete differential geometry** (Crane & Wardetzky) — the source of "The Game" framing; see [DiscreteDifferentialGeometry](../concepts/DiscreteDifferentialGeometry.md).
- **Boundary First Flattening** (Sawhney & Crane) — the piecewise linear strategy for parameterizing conformal maps by boundary data (log conformal factor or geodesic curvature), the one Part I approach with the right flexibility. See [DiscretizationRigidity](../concepts/DiscretizationRigidity.md).
- **Conformal geometry processing** — his Caltech PhD thesis, cited for preliminary work on *extrinsic* conformal maps, an area he flags as still open.
- Applied work he cites his own name on: Möbius Registration, variational surface cutting, and computational design with auxetic materials.

## Position taken in the survey

He grades the discretizations rather than advocating one: [DiscreteConformalEquivalence](../concepts/DiscreteConformalEquivalence.md) is currently the only approach with both the right flexibility *and* a complete uniformization theorem, though [CirclePattern](../concepts/CirclePattern.md)s come very close. He is explicit that exact structure preservation is not automatically superior to fast inexact numerics.
