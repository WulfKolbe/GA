# Feng Luo

The other name central to [DiscreteUniformization](../concepts/DiscreteUniformization.md) in Crane's survey, likewise thanked in the acknowledgements.

## Contributions cited

- **Combinatorial Yamabe flow on surfaces** — introduced the notion of [DiscreteConformalEquivalence](../concepts/DiscreteConformalEquivalence.md) used throughout Part III (independently of Roček & Williams' earlier appearance of the same definition in Regge calculus), together with the flow driving a metric toward constant scalar curvature. No explicit energy was given, and existence held only if the flow avoided singularities. This is the thread that leads to a complete uniformization theorem. See [DiscreteRicciFlow](../concepts/DiscreteRicciFlow.md).
- **Combinatorial Ricci flows on surfaces** (Chow & Luo) — the circle-pattern-based flow, shown to arise from a convex variational principle by examining its Hessian.
- **A discrete uniformization theorem for polyhedral surfaces** I and II (Gu, Luo, Sun, Wu; Gu, Guo, Luo, Sun, Wu) — the **Euclidean** case, and the hyperbolic case from the perspective of discrete conformal equivalence.
- **Rigidity of polyhedral surfaces III** — part of the rigidity results for inversive distance circle packings.
- Discrete heat kernel determines discrete Riemannian metric (Zeng, Guo, Luo, Gu) — one of the two results that establish the Hodge-star rigidity in [DiscretizationRigidity](../concepts/DiscretizationRigidity.md).
