# Boris Springborn

One of the two names most load-bearing for [DiscreteUniformization](../concepts/DiscreteUniformization.md) in Crane's survey; thanked in the acknowledgements for answering questions about it.

## Contributions cited

- **Conformal Equivalence of Triangle Meshes** (Springborn, Schröder, Pinkall) — supplied the first **explicit energy** for conformally equivalent discrete metrics, in terms of the [LobachevskyFunction](../concepts/LobachevskyFunction.md), where [FengLuo](FengLuo.md) had given only a flow. Also the source of the characterization of a discrete conformal class by [LengthCrossRatio](../concepts/LengthCrossRatio.md)s.
- **Discrete conformal maps and ideal hyperbolic polyhedra** (Bobenko, Pinkall, Springborn) — made the connection between discrete conformal equivalence and [IdealHyperbolicPolyhedron](../concepts/IdealHyperbolicPolyhedron.md)s.
- **Variational principles for circle patterns and Koebe's theorem** (Bobenko & Springborn) — connected and generalized the earlier circle-pattern principles, and made them numerically efficient. See [ConvexVariationalPrinciple](../concepts/ConvexVariationalPrinciple.md).
- **Hyperbolic polyhedra and discrete uniformization** — proved the **spherical** case, via an approach that also subsumes the Euclidean and hyperbolic cases; also established that the uniformization energy is $C^2$ everywhere, including across Penner cell boundaries.
- **A variational principle for weighted Delaunay triangulations and hyperideal polyhedra** — part of the hyper-ideal [CirclePattern](../concepts/CirclePattern.md) line that yields uniformization for non-positively curved surfaces.
- A discrete Laplace–Beltrami operator for simplicial surfaces (Bobenko & Springborn) — the finite-flip termination result for [IntrinsicDelaunayTriangulation](../concepts/IntrinsicDelaunayTriangulation.md)s.
