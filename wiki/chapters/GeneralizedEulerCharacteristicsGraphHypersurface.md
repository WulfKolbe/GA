# Generalized Euler Characteristics, Graph Hypersurfaces, and Feynman Periods

**Author:** P. Aluffi

**In:** [Geometric, Algebraic and Topological Methods for Quantum Field Theory](../sources/cardona2013.md), printed p. 95 (PDF p. 106–147)

## Abstract

We give a very informal presentation of background on the Grothendieck group  of varieties and on characteristic classes, both viewed as generalizations of the  ordinary topological Euler characteristic. We then review some recent work  using these tools to study 'graph hypersurfaces'-a topic motivated by the  algebro-geometric interpretation of Feynman amplitudes as periods of comple ments of these hypersurfaces. These notes follow closely, both in content and  style, my lectures at the Summer school in Villa de Leyva, July 5-8, 2011.
