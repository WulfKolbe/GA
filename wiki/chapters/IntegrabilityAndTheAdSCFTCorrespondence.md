# Integrability and the AdS/CFT Correspondence

**Author:** M. Staudacher

**In:** [Geometric, Algebraic and Topological Methods for Quantum Field Theory](../sources/cardona2013.md), printed p. 255 (PDF p. 266–288)

## Abstract

We present a brief, pedagogical introduction to the field of integrability in  gauge and string theory, which has been an intensely researched topic in the  last few years. We point the reader intending to learn the subject to more  extensive reviews of this vast and technically demanding subject.
